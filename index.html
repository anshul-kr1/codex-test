Echoes of Hallownest

Hollow Knight weaves a tale of sacrifice and sleep. These fragments of lore set the tone for your descent.

Sleepers at the Gate

The guardians dream eternal, holding back the plague that gnaws at the heart of Hallownest. Their devotion hums through the ruins.

“We are the vessel. We contain the infection. Our will is cold and pure.”

— Seal of the Dreamers

The Nailsmith's Lament

Forged in fire, tempered in regret. The Nailsmith's craft shaped countless fates, each blade an echo of his ambition.

“I have honed an instrument of death. A masterpiece that haunts me still.”

— The Nailsmith

Kingdom of Moths

Once radiant, now hushed. The Moth Tribe tend the light of dreams, guiding lost spirits through the blackened halls of Hallownest.

“In dreams we bear witness. In waking we endure the stillness.”

— Seer of the Resting Grounds
